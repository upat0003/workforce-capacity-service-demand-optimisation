# Privacy Impact Assessment

## Scope

This assessment covers the synthetic client, employee and service-delivery data used across
the workforce capacity and service-demand optimisation platform. No real personal information
is used anywhere in this repository; all records are generated by
`python/ingestion/generate_synthetic_data.py` with a fixed random seed. This assessment is
written as if the same design were applied to real operational data, because that is the
purpose of documenting it.

## Data subjects and categories of data

| Data subject | Data categories held | Special-category risk |
|---|---|---|
| Clients | Pseudonymous client ID, region, complexity tier, preferred language, communication preference, consent flag | Health/support-need proxy via complexity tier and service type (e.g. nursing, behaviour support) |
| Employees | Pseudonymous employee ID, role, employment type, contracted hours, base location, gender, age band, hourly-rate band | Gender and age band are protected attributes; held only for fairness monitoring |

## Design controls

1. **No real names or contact details are modelled.** Clients and employees are identified only
   by system-generated IDs (`CLT-#####`, `EMP-#####`). This is a deliberate design choice, not an
   artefact of the demo, so the pattern generalises to a real deployment with a proper
   pseudonymisation layer in front of the analytics platform.
2. **Consent gating.** `gold.dim_client` is built with a `WHERE consent_data_use_flag = true`
   filter (`sql/gold/02_star_schema.sql`) -- clients who have not consented to data use for
   planning/analytics purposes are excluded from every downstream gold table, feature set and
   model.
3. **Protected-attribute exclusion from models.** `gender`, `age_band`, `preferred_language` and
   bilingual-role status are held only on `gold.dim_employee_governed` and are never joined into
   any feature table under `python/features/` or `data/processed/`. They are used exclusively by
   `python/governance/fairness_checks.py` to test for disparate impact after the fact. This is
   enumerated explicitly in `configs/access_control.yml` under
   `protected_attributes_excluded_from_models`.
4. **Small-number suppression.** `gold.mart_fairness_workload` suppresses any cohort with fewer
   than 8 employees (`HAVING count(*) >= 8`), and `python/governance/fairness_checks.py`
   re-applies the same floor from `configs/monitoring.yml`, to prevent re-identification through
   small cross-tabulations.
5. **Row-level access scoping.** Regional coordinators are scoped to their assigned region via
   the `row_level_filter` in `configs/access_control.yml`; see
   [`access_control_matrix.md`](access_control_matrix.md).
6. **Retention.** Raw service-request and appointment detail is retained for 24 months
   operationally and then aggregated to the gold marts only; see the retention table below.

## Retention schedule

| Data category | Retention (operational) | Retention (aggregated) |
|---|---|---|
| Client service requests / appointments / outcomes | 24 months from appointment date | Indefinite at `gold.mart_*` aggregate grain |
| Employee shift and overtime detail | 24 months | Indefinite at aggregate grain |
| Model predictions and scoring logs | 12 months | N/A |
| Override audit log | 7 years (compliance/incident-review requirement) | N/A |

## Residual risk and mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Re-identification of a client via a rare complexity/region/service-type combination | Low | Medium | Small-cohort suppression on any cross-tabulated export; access restricted to `regional_coordinator` and above |
| Protected attribute leaking into a model via a correlated proxy feature (e.g. preferred_language correlating with region) | Medium | Medium | Fairness checks run on every pipeline execution (`python/governance/fairness_checks.py`); any breach is logged to `artifacts/monitoring/fairness_report.json` and escalated per `risk_register.md` |
| Over-broad analyst access to raw PII-adjacent fields | Low | Medium | Role-based access control matrix; analysts are scoped to `silver`/`gold`, not `bronze.employees` or `bronze.clients` |

## Sign-off

This assessment is reviewed at each major model or schema change and at minimum annually,
consistent with the review cadence in `governance/model_card.md`.
